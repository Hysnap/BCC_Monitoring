Sutton Walmley and Minworth Ward results 2026

A poll was held for the election of councillors to Birmingham City Council for Sutton Walmley and Minworth Ward.

The poll took place on Thursday 7 May 2026 between 7:00am and 10:00pm.

The number of councillors elected for this ward is 2.

The following people were nominated for election as a councillor for this ward.
